Frequently Asked Questions

The following FAQs contain answers to the questions most commonly asked by prospective and current members of APU Kickers. Should your question be unanswered here, please refer to the Contact and Enquiry page or submit your question via the Feedback Portal.

Membership Eligibility

Who can become a member of APU Kickers?

All current students of APU, irrespective of their program of study or semester, are eligible to join the club. It is unnecessary to have prior experience playing for either a club or school team to join — beginners are welcome, provided that they commit to attending regular training sessions and working collaboratively with the team.

How can I register as a member?

To register as a member, you must complete the registration form located on the Join Us page prior to the intake deadline. Upon successful submission of the form, you will receive an email confirming your registration and providing information regarding the next training session, including a link to the club group chat. Alternatively, you may approach any committee member during a training session at the APU Sport Centre.

Is there an annual membership fee?

Yes, a small semester-based fee is charged to cover expenses related to pitch rental, match balls, bibs, first-aid equipment, and league registration. The exact amount of the current semester's fee is posted on the Join Us page and within the club group chat. Membership fees are collected once per semester and are non-refundable once paid.

Does one need to participate in a trial or selection process?

There is no formal trial process for general members. However, players interested in being considered for the competitive match-day squad will undergo assessment during training sessions by the coach and team captain. Selection is determined based on factors such as attendance, attitude, physical fitness, and teamwork, rather than merely skill level.

Can someone join mid-semester?

Yes, individuals wishing to join mid-semester are typically permitted to do so up until the registration cutoff date established each semester. Even if an individual misses this cutoff date, they are still invited to train with the club; however, they may not qualify for league matches until the following semester.

Training Sessions

When and where do training sessions occur?

Training occurs every Tuesday at 4:00 PM at the APU Sport Centre. Additional optional fitness or tactical sessions may be scheduled prior to major matches and will be communicated to members via the club group chat at least three days in advance.

How long do individual training sessions typically last?

Individual training sessions typically last approximately two hours, encompassing a warm-up period, technical drills, tactical exercises, a small-sided game, and a cool-down period. Please arrive at least 10 minutes prior to the scheduled start time to ensure timely commencement of the session.

What items should I bring with me to training sessions?

You should bring your own football boots, shin guards, a water bottle, and a towel. The club provides training bibs, cones, balls, and a basic first-aid kit. A comprehensive list of required attire and equipment can be found on the Attire & Equipment page.

What occurs if it rains during a training session?

In general, light rainfall will not result in cancellation of a training session. However, if conditions become dangerous due to adverse weather or if the sports centre determines that the pitch is unsuitable for use, the session will be canceled or relocated indoors. Notification of such changes will be disseminated through the club group chat at least one hour prior to the scheduled start time.

Is participation in training sessions mandatory?

Participation in training sessions is not mandatory for general members; however, regular participation is expected from players seeking selection for matches. If a member is unable to participate in a training session, they should notify the team captain or a member of the committee in advance.

Matches & Events

How will I know when the next match is scheduled?

Match fixtures, locations, and kick-off times are published on the Match Schedule page and in the Event Calendar. Additionally, reminders will be distributed through the club group chat several days prior to each match.

Can non-members attend matches as spectators?

Yes. APU students and staff are encouraged to attend matches as spectators and support the team. We only request that spectators remain outside of the playing field and exhibit respectful behavior toward both teams and the referee at all times.

In which competitions does the club compete?

Throughout the academic year, the club competes in various inter-university and internal APU leagues and tournaments. Additionally, the club occasionally organizes friendly matches against other clubs. A historical record of the club's accomplishments can be accessed on the Club Honours page.

Are social events organized in addition to matches?

Yes. The club organizes watch parties for major football matches, friendly futsal nights, and end-of-semester social gatherings. Information regarding upcoming social events and registration links will be disseminated through the Event Calendar and the club group chat.

Attire, Equipment & Safety

Is there a specific club uniform that I must wear?

For training sessions, any comfortable athletic apparel is acceptable; however, shin guards are required. For official matches, players are required to wear the club jersey, which can be purchased from the Merchandise Store.

What actions should I take if I sustain an injury during a training session?

If you sustain an injury during a training session, you should cease participation immediately and notify either the coach or the closest member of the committee. A basic first-aid kit is available at every training session; more severe injuries can be addressed by personnel employed by the APU Sport Centre. Any injury sustained during a training session should be documented in the club's incident log.

Is a medical declaration required?

Yes. Prior to registration as a member of APU Kickers, all applicants must complete a brief health and emergency-contact declaration form. You should also inform the committee of any allergies, asthma, heart conditions or previous injuries that may impact your ability to participate.

Am I allowed to borrow club equipment?

Club-owned training equipment is available for use during official training sessions only and must be returned to the equipment officer at the conclusion of each training session. Personal items such as boots, shin guards, and water bottles are not supplied by the club.

Membership, Feedback & General Information

Can I serve on the committee?

Yes. At the conclusion of each term, nominations for committee positions such as president, secretary, treasurer, events officer and media officer are solicited from active members in good standing. Any active member in good standing may run for election to a committee position.

How can I provide feedback or file a complaint?

You can provide feedback or file a complaint by utilizing the Feedback Portal. Comments, suggestions or complaints submitted through this portal will be reviewed by the committee; serious complaints will be discussed with the club adviser. You may also directly address your concerns with any member of the committee.

Who has access to my personal information?

Personal information collected during registration will only be utilized for administrative purposes of the club, emergency contacts and league registration. Your personal information will not be shared with third parties or utilized for promotional activities.

How can I contact APU Kickers?

You can contact APU Kickers via the Contact and Enquiry page, by contacting a member of the committee during a training session, or via the club's social media accounts listed on the News & Media page.
